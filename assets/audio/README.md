# Exploratory audio

The slice's audio is **exploratory atmosphere**, not the M18 soundtrack. It exists so
places and actions can be heard now, and so the project has a rights-clean pipeline when
final audio is commissioned.

## What ships

| Kind | Count | Files |
|---|---|---|
| Ambience | 10 | open water, Ugarit harbour/market/temple/workshops/night, Cypriot yard/smithy/inn/night |
| Music | 2 | the title theme, a wordless temple sketch |
| One-shot effects | 4 | weighed silver, hammer, oar, gate |

The catalog is `src/ui/audio.ts`; the licensing ledger is `manifest.json`; the files are
in `public/audio/`.

## Sources and licenses

The field recordings are **CC0** from [BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com/), created
by Joseph SARDIN. They are modern recordings used as material for atmosphere; they are
not evidence for how a Bronze Age harbor, market, workshop or temple actually sounded.

- [Gulls on the Harbor](https://bigsoundbank.com/gulls-on-the-harbor-s2573.html)
- [Beach: Small Waves](https://bigsoundbank.com/beach-small-waves-s0265.html)
- [Outdoor Market #1](https://bigsoundbank.com/outdoor-market-1-s2728.html)
- [Fireplace #1](https://bigsoundbank.com/fireplace-1-s0030.html)
- [Anvil #1](https://bigsoundbank.com/anvil-1-s3589.html)
- [Field Cricket](https://bigsoundbank.com/field-cricket-s1020.html)
- [Frogs #1](https://bigsoundbank.com/frogs-1-s0997.html)

Each track was trimmed, filtered, looped and mixed for this project; those changes are
recorded per asset in `manifest.json`. The anvil is a modern metal surface, not a claim
about a Bronze Age anvil.

The two music cues and all four effects are **original procedural synthesis** generated by
`tools/build-audio.mjs` and dedicated to CC0 by this project. The music is a modal sketch
for lyre-like plucks and frame drum. It is deliberately **not** a reconstruction of the
Hurrian Hymn, a surviving melody, or a known performance practice, and no text or invented
language is sung.

## Rebuild and check

```bash
pnpm audio:build   # needs ffmpeg and network; writes public/audio + manifest.json
pnpm audio:check   # offline: files, IDs, loop flags, licenses, provenance
```

`audio:build` caches downloaded sources in `.cache/audio/`, which is ignored. The built
MP3s and `manifest.json` are committed so a playthrough needs neither ffmpeg nor the
network. `pnpm check` includes `audio:check`.

## Direction

- Diegetic first: gulls, water, work, fire, voices, a temple hush.
- No modern port machinery, trailer impacts, cinematic drones or fantasy choir.
- Music belongs to the title and the temple; other places are carried by ambience.
- The market and inn murmurs are deliberately not intelligible modern speech.
- Missing or blocked audio must never block play; the visible **Sound on/off** control
  persists per device, and the game remains fully playable muted.
