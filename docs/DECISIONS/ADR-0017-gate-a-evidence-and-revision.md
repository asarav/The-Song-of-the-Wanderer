# ADR-0017 — Gate A on a design-owner pass, and a revision pass

**Status:** accepted · 2026-11-27
**Related:** `docs/milestones/M4-slice-evaluation.md`, `docs/milestones/M4b-slice-revision.md`, `docs/playtests/human/2026-11-27-design-owner-quick-pass.md`, `docs/playtests/findings.md`

## Context

M4-T01 asked for three recorded human sessions — trader, curious traveller and helper —
with at least one player who had not read the design docs. The design owner played the
slice instead, supplied twenty-five notes covering controls, market, systems clarity,
conversations, maps, journal and audio, and declared the playtest done. No second or third
persona was run, no video was made, and the person playing wrote the design.

Holding M4 open indefinitely would block all work on a protocol the owner does not intend
to run now. Treating the pass as if it met the protocol would overstate the evidence and
hide a real gap from the next agent.

## Decision

- **Accept the evidence we have for Gate A:** one design-owner pass recorded as detailed
  notes, three agent sessions, and the fixes those produced. The milestone records the
  original three-session protocol as **not met**, not as satisfied.
- **Decision: revise**, not expand. The evidence is strong on the world and the travel
  loop, and weak on character motivation, faction legibility and the first twenty minutes.
  Those are fixed before regional expansion, per “the slice comes before the world”.
- **Define a short M4b** with the top three blockers. One of its acceptance conditions is a
  recorded session by a reader who has not seen the design docs, so the missing external
  evidence is obtained before M5 rather than dropped.
- **M5–M8 remain blocked** until M4b’s acceptance criteria are met.
- The waiver is visible in the milestone: the unmet M4-T01 items stay on the page with the
  reason, rather than being ticked.

## Consequences

- External validity is weaker than the milestone intended. The design owner knows the
  intended reading of every screen; the fresh-reader session in M4b is the remedy, and
  findings written from this pass are labelled accordingly.
- Character depth and the opening are scheduled before world scale. M5 (world model and
  timeline) is not started on top of a cast that does not yet carry a scene.
- The revise decision is a scheduling choice, not a rejection of the slice: the world,
  crossing and trade loop are kept as they are.
- If the owner overrules the revise decision, a superseding ADR may open M5 before M4b is
  complete. That is a deliberate choice to carry the known weaknesses into Wave 1, and it
  must be recorded as one.
