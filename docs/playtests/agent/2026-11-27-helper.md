## Story of the session

I opened with forty silver, let Hannu look at me without giving a name, and went to the workshop quarter instead of the market. Sibtu asked me to sell her cloth; I spent a day, took the bundle, sold it for five shekels, and put all five back in her hand. The journal recorded the debt, the sale and the repayment, but her thanks were deliberately withheld. I bought grain for the crossing, paid the coastal fare, bailed during a squall, paid a ten-shekel toll, and reached Cyprus. Shipti's inn conversation again came before the island description. I paid for a room, then bought grain and haggled Kinyras to thirty-six shekels. The counter-offer paid more but left no copper-deal entry or deed; the letter arrived two days later. The promised consequence of helping Sibtu existed in the save as `sibtu.household-settled`, but there was no journal entry or scene for it.

## Bugs

### B1 — The Cyprus arrival still starts at the inn

- Severity: major
- Where: first arrival at the Cypriot harbor
- Steps:
  1. Travel from Ugarit to Cyprus.
  2. Read the arrival scene, then the destination screen.
- Expected: landfall and harbor first.
- Actual: Shipti's doorway scene first; “The island comes up gray and long” afterwards.
- Evidence: the same sequence occurred on both playthroughs.
- Notes: fixed by moving the landfall into the arrival scene and making the destination intro a present-tense place description.

### B2 — The counter-offer branch loses the deal record

- Severity: major
- Where: Kinyras, scheduled offer, “Thirty-six” branch
- Steps:
  1. Meet Kinyras, gain his respect, leave, spend a day.
  2. Choose the thirty-six-shekel counter-offer.
  3. Take the silver and wait for the letter.
- Expected: the same journal, deed, faction trust and knowledge as the twenty-four-shekel deal, plus the better price.
- Actual: only the money changed; no copper-deal journal, no deed, no merchant trust, no knowledge fact.
- Evidence: the journal had “Copper and white walls” and “A letter with a seal” with no “A price agreed” between them.
- Notes: fixed; `scenario.slice.cyprus-counter` now covers the branch.

### B3 — News of Sibtu could claim a settlement that never happened

- Severity: major
- Where: scheduled `evt.cyprus.arrival-news`, eight days after taking the cloth
- Steps:
  1. Take Sibtu's cloth.
  2. Leave Ugarit without returning the five shekels.
  3. Reach Cyprus and wait eight days.
- Expected: the “household settled” consequence only if the money was returned.
- Actual: the event was gated on `journal.slice.refugee-helped`, which is set when the bundle is taken, not when the five shekels are paid.
- Evidence: the event condition in `cyprus-arrival-news.json`.
- Notes: fixed by gating on `journal.slice.sibtu-cloth-settled`. The event now also writes a journal entry, `journal.slice.sibtu-news`, so the good outcome is visible rather than a hidden knowledge fact.

### B4 — Paying for a room changed nothing

- Severity: minor
- Where: Shipti's inn, “Take the room”, 3 shekels
- Steps:
  1. Arrive on Cyprus with at least three shekels.
  2. Ask the price of a room, then take it.
- Expected: a night's rest and some benefit.
- Actual: three shekels disappeared; no day passed and no reserve was restored.
- Evidence: the choice's only effect was `money: -3`.
- Notes: fixed; the copy now says one night, and the choice advances a day, restores two reserve and adds one trust with Shipti.

## Ideas

### I1 — Make the delayed kindness visible

- Pillar: characters remember.
- Player problem: repaying Sibtu changed a hidden fact and a faction number, but the player saw only the absence of thanks.
- Evidence: the final journal had no entry for the news; the knowledge fact was `told` but unfindable as a scene.
- Cost: content (done).
- Sketch: the new “Word from the workshop quarter” journal entry gives the paid branch a visible return without adding a new mechanic.

### I2 — Give the night kiln a second, shorter beat

- Pillar: a lived-in world.
- Player problem: the same long Ada conversation returns after the cooldown.
- Evidence: the kiln scene appeared again on day 12.
- Cost: content.
- Sketch: after the first visit, a two-line “the kiln is banked; Ada nods” variant, or a shorter entry node once `ada.met` is set.

Sibtu is the strongest character beat in the slice: the request is specific, the five shekels are a real choice, and the paid ending's withheld thanks lands because the journal explains it. The crossing is still the weakest stretch; the squall, the toll and the night at anchor are well written, but the toll's only affordable answer is another day of work, and the language check never becomes a route to a better outcome in this run. The new journal consequence makes the helper route feel remembered instead of merely tallied. The world remains distinctive, especially the Cypriot yard and the workshop quarter.

## Recommendation

Keep the Sibtu thread as the slice's emotional spine, and make every completed branch leave a visible record. The counter-offer fix and the news entry are the minimum; a shorter repeat for the night kiln would remove the one moment that felt like filler.
