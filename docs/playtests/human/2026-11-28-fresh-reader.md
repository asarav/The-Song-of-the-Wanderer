## What is attested

this build. The reader began at Begin, played through the opening and the Ugarit arrival,
and reached the first crossing. After the session the reader could explain, without being
told, what grain and reserve are for and what the crossing fare buys.

This file records those acceptance points as the owner's attestation. It is not a
transcript, and it contains no quotes, timestamps or observations attributed to the
reader. Notes supplied by the reader replace this attestation if they are pasted before
the build; none were supplied.

the design docs. This attestation satisfies that condition for M4b-T02, and the milestone
is closed on it. It does not replace the three-persona protocol that ADR-0017 records as
unmet, and it is not evidence for M5 or later.
